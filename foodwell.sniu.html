How we make profit.

We do not depend on a single bet. foodwell.sniu runs four independent strategies at the same time, so a weak year in one market is covered by strength in another.

Global Macro

We trade currencies, rates and commodities based on how economies and policy are moving.

Equity Long/Short

We buy strong companies and short weak ones, earning from the gap between them.

Quantitative

Our models scan millions of data points daily to find small, repeatable edges.

Event-Driven

We invest around mergers, restructurings and other events with clear outcomes.
